# Chapter landscape card study

Separate live study: public/gartenkarten.html, linked from the garden catalogue study. Three decorative chapter landscapes reuse actual frame, earth, meadow, flower, stone, fence and living-water modules. No catalogue puzzle or progress data is edited.

- Am Gartenzaun: irregular compact island, honey picket fence, earth clearing and daisy/poppy beds.
- Zwischen den Ufern: two green banks separated by a bent blue basin; daisy/buttercup planting.
- Schmale Pfade: offset narrow path surrounded by individual mineral/leaf stones; meadow/buttercup planting. No fused rocks.

Wide landscape panels sit above chapter name and action. The unvisited view retains a green base and sparse flowers; three native growth controls vary flower density for comparison. This simulated density is not connected to actual progress yet. Buttons open a native study information dialog, not the matching chapter. Menus/game remain unchanged apart from the link to this separate study.

Camera bounds fit actual geometry with padding, tilted view and consistent upper-left lighting.
