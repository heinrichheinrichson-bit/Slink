# Expanded catalogue

The browser now loads `slink-expansion-levels.js` after the original and fence catalogues. It adds 600 certified puzzles (200 terrain layouts and 400 fence layouts), then orders all 1200 puzzles into 29 gardens using stable IDs. The original 600 structural puzzle definitions, terrain, decoration seeds and IDs are retained. Presentation metadata and order intentionally change. The 112-puzzle archive and twelve separate Denkpfade remain untouched.

The first 30 tutorial puzzles and ten compact fence introductions remain first. Remaining boards are classified by delayed wrong choices, with increasing score inside each category. Categories have at most 50 puzzles, balanced within each difficulty band rather than leaving a three-puzzle remainder. Both terrain and fences appear throughout the later bands. No weak puzzle is deleted merely for its difficulty; it moves to an earlier band.

Expert categories require at least seven delayed wrong choices and at most 79% locally forced moves. The final garden requires at least eleven delayed wrong choices. A delayed wrong choice survives at least three moves after connectivity and degree pruning. The audit searches bounded wrong branches (ten moves/600 nodes), so these are selection heuristics, not calibrated human difficulty. Human playtests remain necessary.

New fences are generated by blocking alternative solutions, then removing redundant barriers. All 2187 retained new fence edges are essential: removing any one permits another exact solution. New fence candidates require at least four delayed wrong choices, wrong-branch depth at least five, forced ratio <=78%, forced run <=12. Board size remains at most seven columns. Graph novelty ignores goals and cosmetics and normalizes translation, reflection and rotation.

`npm test` includes exact uniqueness and graph novelty for all new boards, unchanged old structural data, category thresholds, old numeric-progress migration, all 1200 gameplay routes/hints and fence rejection. Main home, picker, native Android asset list and rendering fixture load the same catalogue. No new APK is produced by this change.

## Reproduction

`npm run catalogue:expand` runs the deterministic fence generator, then assembly. Assembly requires the original ignored `work/pool6.json` and `work/pool7.json` candidate pools; regenerate these with `node public/tools/catalogue-curator.cjs generate 6 120000 work/pool6.json` and `node public/tools/catalogue-curator.cjs generate 7 160000 work/pool7.json` if absent. Do not rerun the original catalogue installer, which replaces baseline files. Candidate pools remain ignored; installed puzzle data and the audit report are versioned.

IDs and completed-ID records remain stable; displayed puzzle numbers and garden positions change. The legacy numeric mapping still references the original 500 IDs. Saved reviews and hint budgets continue to use IDs.
